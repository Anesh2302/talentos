Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Is TalentOS really free?▼

Yes! The Starter plan is completely free with no time limit. You can create profiles, apply to jobs, and use basic features at no cost. Premium plans unlock advanced tools for teams.

How does face recognition work?▼

Face recognition runs entirely in your browser using TensorFlow.js. When you enroll, a unique mathematical descriptor of your face is created and stored securely. Your actual image is never sent to our servers — only the encrypted numerical descriptor.

Can I use it on mobile?▼

Yes! TalentOS is fully responsive and works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge on both desktop and mobile devices. The face recognition feature works with front-facing cameras on phones.

How is my data protected?▼

All data is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Passwords are hashed using scrypt. Face descriptors are stored as encrypted numerical vectors — your actual face image is never stored on our servers after enrollment.

Can I switch plans later?▼

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. All your data is preserved when switching plans. We offer a 14-day free trial on all paid plans with no commitment.
